## 1.0.1

**Small update. No gameplay changes.**

- The in-game mod list (Mods → EnviroMine Revived) now links to the
  [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Jom3a-J/EnviroMine-Revived).
- If the game crashes with this mod, the crash screen now points to the
  [issue tracker](https://github.com/Jom3a-J/EnviroMine-Revived/issues).

Your saved stats and config files from 1.0.0 carry over as they are. Replace the 1.0.0 jar with
this one on both the client and the server.

Requires Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.267 or newer.

## 1.0.0

**First release.** Body temperature and hydration for Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge, a
vanilla-plus port of the classic 1.7.10 mod EnviroMine.

- Body temperature that follows the biome, time of day, weather, height, nearby heat and cold,
  water and what you wear.
- Hydration that drains over about an hour, with clean, cold, dirty and salty water. Boil water
  in a cauldron over a fire to make it clean.
- Gentle, telegraphed consequences: a warning first, then a mild effect, then slow damage.
- A compact HUD above the hunger bar that you can move, hide or switch to numbers.
- A server config for every balance number, and data packs for block heat, armour and biomes.

Requires Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.267 or newer, on both client and server.
