## Possible later work (not in v1)

From the original EnviroMine, explicitly out of scope for v1:

- Block physics and cave-ins
- Gas pockets and air quality (gas masks)
- Sanity
- Caving hazards
- Food spoilage
- Earthquakes
- Achievement pages (could return as advancements)

Other ideas:

- Frostbite (gentler, never permanent)
- Camel pack / canteen
- Per-dimension temperature rules
- Compatibility with other mods' seasons or climate systems
